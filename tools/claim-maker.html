Amble Claim Maker

    Pick a spot, then drag its pin, click the map or search to move it. Every change saves into data/claim-quests.json.
    This page saves through the quest maker server. Run bun tools/quest-maker-server.ts, then open http://localhost:8744/claim.

    Spots …

      Where

      Moving a spot also moves it in Claim games already running, on the next deploy.

      Challenge

      "At the spot" checks the proof photo was taken inside the circle. "Anywhere" skips that check, and the app leaves the spot off its maps and lists it last, so name it something like "Anywhere on campus".

      Score

      The best score holds the spot. A new unit only reaches new games; running ones keep theirs.
